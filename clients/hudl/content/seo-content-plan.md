# SEO Content Plan — HUDL Consultancy
_Created: 2026-06-04_

Six article ideas to support HUDL's organic growth across its core service areas. Each targets a clear keyword with commercial or informational intent that maps to a specific service and stage of the buyer journey.

---

## Article 1 — PRIORITY: Draft first
**Working title:** What Does a Marketing Consultancy Actually Do? (UK Guide)
**Target keyword:** marketing consultancy UK
**Search intent:** Informational / navigational — decision-makers researching whether to hire a consultancy vs agency or in-house team
**Service supported:** Brand strategy + positioning (all services; drives consultation bookings)
**Angle:** Cut through the confusion between "agency" and "consultancy" — HUDL is outcome-led, not retainer-padded. Explains what a UK marketing consultancy delivers, who it's right for, and the commercial case for hiring one.
**Category:** industry-insights

---

## Article 2
**Working title:** Google Ads for Small Business: A No-Nonsense UK Guide
**Target keyword:** Google Ads for small business UK
**Search intent:** Informational / commercial — SME owners weighing whether to start or improve PPC
**Service supported:** Paid media / Google Ads (PPC)
**Angle:** Strip away the jargon and give UK SME owners a plain-English framework — what to expect, what it costs (illustrative ranges), and the five things to get right before spending a penny.
**Category:** marketing-tips

---

## Article 3
**Working title:** Brand Strategy for SMEs: Why It's Not Just a Logo
**Target keyword:** brand strategy for small business UK
**Search intent:** Informational — founders and MDs who know they have a brand problem but aren't sure what fixing it involves
**Service supported:** Brand strategy
**Angle:** Brand isn't aesthetics — it's the commercial decision-making filter. Walk through what a proper brand strategy covers, what it unlocks for paid and organic channels, and what HUDL's process looks like.
**Category:** industry-insights

---

## Article 4
**Working title:** Performance Marketing Explained: What It Is and Why It Matters
**Target keyword:** performance marketing UK
**Search intent:** Informational — marketing leads and MDs unclear on how performance marketing differs from traditional spend
**Service supported:** Performance marketing / analytics
**Angle:** Define performance marketing clearly, explain the metrics that actually matter (CPA, ROAS, CLV — not impressions), and show why tying every channel to commercial outcomes changes how budgets get allocated.
**Category:** marketing-tips

---

## Article 5
**Working title:** Marketing for Removals Companies: How to Win More Leads Online
**Target keyword:** marketing for removals companies / removals company marketing UK
**Search intent:** Informational / commercial — owners of UK removals and relocation businesses looking for specialist marketing help
**Service supported:** Paid media + SEO + content (sector: removals/relocation)
**Angle:** Specific, sector-focused guide for the removals sector — the buyer journey is short and high-intent, so PPC and local SEO dominate. Draw on HUDL's removals/relocation client experience (Abels, Bishop's Move) to illustrate what works. Use illustrative figures only.
**Category:** case-study

---

## Article 6
**Working title:** How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in the UK (2026 Guide)
**Target keyword:** how to choose a digital marketing agency UK
**Search intent:** Informational / commercial — buyers comparing agencies, building shortlists
**Service supported:** All services / new business
**Angle:** A genuinely useful buying guide — red flags, the right questions to ask, the difference between a generalist agency and a sector-specialist consultancy, and how to evaluate proposals. Positions HUDL without being a thinly veiled sales pitch.
**Category:** marketing-tips

---

## Notes
- Articles 1–3 are the highest-priority for search volume and direct-to-consultation conversion.
- Article 5 (removals sector) is a strong short-term win: low competition, highly specific, supports three existing clients.
- Read time targets: 4–6 min read (800–1,200 words each).
- Internal linking opportunities: each new post should link back to at least one existing post.
