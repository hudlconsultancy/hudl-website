## About AGM Relocation Limited

AGM Relocation Limited is the **parent company** operating **four distinct removal
and relocation brands** in the UK: **Abels Moving Services**, **Bishop's Move**,
**Gerson Relocation** and **Gerson Moving Services (GMS)**. The group spans domestic
removals, international relocation and corporate mobility. Some brands trace their
origins back centuries.

Budgets, strategy and agency relationships are managed at **group level by Woody**
(Craig Wood, Fractional Marketing Director via HUDL Consultancy), with brand-level
execution overseen by individual marketing managers. Woody interfaces directly with
AGM senior leadership: Mark Costa-Rising, Neil Pertoldi, James Sawyer, Louise
Dunckley and Heather Darby.

## Brand 1: Abels Moving Services
- **Positioning:** Premium domestic & international removals. Holds a **Royal Warrant** — a significant, underutilised differentiator. Reference it as a mark of exceptional quality, with care and in compliance with Royal Warrant guidelines. Never decorative.
- **Market:** Premium tier; primary competitor (via auction insights) is **AnyVan**, which holds significant impression share. Positioned against mid-market aggregators and direct firms.
- **Depots:** Multiple UK locations including Ely (CB6).
- **Services:** Domestic house removals, international removals & shipping, European removals, storage, packing/unpacking, fine art & antiques handling.
- **Digital:** WordPress site (dev: **NetDesigner**). PPC & SEO historically **JDR Group** (relationship under active review; Woody taking direct account management during a pause). Technical SEO legacy via NetMatters (no longer active).
- **Google Ads:** Campaign structure — Domestic, London, Europe Outbound, International. tCPA bid strategy; budget-constrained vs efficiency-constrained diagnostic framework. Location bid modifiers from 80-day data; zero-impression locations removed; ad schedule adjustments applied. Performance Max in use. **Customer Match** list from Moveware CRM export (399 rows, 326 usable hashed records) as an audience signal. Dedicated PPC domestic-removals landing page developed (v1–v5) with real brand photography and Royal Warrant compliance.
- **Tone:** Premium, trusted, heritage-led. Expertise, care, premium service. Never aggregator-style language.

## Brand 2: Bishop's Move
- **Positioning:** One of the UK's oldest removal companies — **founded 1854**. Strong reputation for quality and reliability. Sits above mid-market aggregators.
- **Services:** Domestic removals, international removals & freight forwarding, European moves, storage (short/long-term), packing, commercial/office moves, fine art & specialist handling.
- **Digital:** Paid media by **MCM Digital** (~£6,725/month actuals). **HUDL retainer £3,000/month.** Performance Max in use, identified as **budget-constrained** (scaling budget is the priority over target adjustment). Marketing budget structured July 2026–June 2027.
- **Analytics:** GA4. Session-to-lead conversion uses **summed individual key-event rates, not blended**. GA4 Cross-Network channel = Performance Max traffic. Divisional leads calculator + back-of-envelope PPC budget calculator built for stakeholder planning.
- **Stakeholders:** **Louise Dunckley** (key internal), **James Comley** (budget/commercial).
- **Tone:** Heritage, trust, dependability. 1854 is the key proof point — established without feeling dated.

## Brand 3: Gerson Relocation
- **Positioning:** Corporate & international mobility specialist. **Dual audience:** the business buyer (HR / mobility / procurement) and the relocating employee.
- **Services:** International employee relocation, corporate mobility programmes, destination services (settling-in, school search, area orientation), home search, immigration support coordination, tenancy management, cultural training, group moves.
- **Digital:** Website **rebuild in progress** with **CoderKube** (selected via RFP; Fast Generations and Netdesignr not selected). PPC by **MCM Digital** — phased proof-of-concept budget anchored to Jan 2026 performance, cost-per-job North Star. Dual-audience wireframes built (corporate pathway + employee pathway). 15-slide RFP deck + full homepage developer handoff.
- **Tone:** Professional, expert, reassuring. Flex registers: confidence in process/compliance for corporate buyers; empathy and clarity for relocating employees. Clarity is a differentiator — avoid corporate jargon.
- *(See also `clients/gerson/personas-positioning.md` for the 10-persona reference.)*

## Brand 4: Gerson Moving Services (GMS)
- **Positioning:** Domestic / consumer removals brand under the Gerson name. Serves private individuals and families — **distinct from Gerson Relocation**.
- **Services:** Domestic house removals, packing/unpacking, storage, European consumer moves, man-and-van + full-service options (positioning TBC).
- **Planning:** Budget modelled as a **combined Gerson/GMS structure** with scenario planning.
- **Tone:** Friendly, reliable, accessible — more approachable than corporate Gerson Relocation. Must feel distinct even though it shares the Gerson name.

## Writing standards (per brand)
- **Abels:** Premium, Royal Warrant-compliant, heritage-led. Never aggregator language.
- **Bishop's Move:** Heritage (1854), trusted, established. Founding date is a key proof point.
- **Gerson Relocation:** Professional/corporate for B2B; empathetic and clear for B2C employees.
- **GMS:** Approachable, friendly, accessible. Distinct in tone from Gerson Relocation.

Across all brands: avoid generic removal-industry copy. Each brand has a distinct
positioning and must not sound interchangeable with competitors — or with each other.
