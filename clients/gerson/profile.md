## Voice & guardrails
- Tone: premium, corporate, global — assured and professional; for B2C, calm, educational and reassuring ("we'll guide you", "we've done this many times before").
- Do: emphasise consistency, control, governance and white-glove service for B2B; reduce stress and give process clarity for B2C.
- Do: use **cost logic and ranges, never exact prices** (B2C personas fear hidden costs; procurement wants transparency).
- Don't: sound like a budget domestic mover; don't be salesy with B2C researchers — they want reassurance, not a pitch.
- Compliance notes: any quoted prices/savings need a real source.
