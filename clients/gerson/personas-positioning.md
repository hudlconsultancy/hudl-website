# Gerson Relocation — Persona & Positioning Reference

> Source: client-supplied "B2B and B2C Persona & Positioning Document". Agents should use this to ground messaging, content and targeting for Gerson. Figures (ages, gender split, origin cities) are from the client's GA data as stated in the source — treat as client-provided, not independently verified.

## Positioning in one line
A **strategic relocation partner delivering consistency, control, and confidence worldwide** — operating as a decision-shaping authority, not a lead form.

## Service-line positioning
- **Corporate Relocation Programmes** — fully managed global relocation with single accountability.
- **Destination Services** — employee-focused support that reduces stress and accelerates productivity.
- **Mobility Consultancy** — strategic advisory for policy, cost optimisation, and governance.

## B2C personas (web-behaviour led)

### 1. Corporate Assignee (Company-Managed)
- **Funding:** company-expensed, budget-approved, supplier chosen by employer/framework.
- **Profile (GA):** age 25–44; balanced gender (~53% M / 47% F); origins London, Birmingham, Cardiff; tech / finance / professional services.
- **Motivation:** reduce stress, understand the process, feel in control despite not choosing the supplier.
- **Pain points:** lack of process clarity, fear of delays/customs, feeling like "a number".
- **Website use:** destination pros & cons, cost-of-living comparison, high video engagement; seeking reassurance not sales.
- **Conversion trigger:** clear explanation of the relocation journey, human tone, "we've done this many times before".

### 2. Self-Funded International Professional
- **Funding:** fully self-funded (occasionally reimbursed later).
- **Profile:** age 25–39; origins London (strongest), Dublin; remote workers, consultants, digital professionals.
- **Motivation:** lifestyle improvement, career progression, freedom/flexibility.
- **Pain points:** hidden costs, what to ship vs sell, choosing the right partner.
- **Website use:** "Pros & Cons of Living in X", "Moving to the USA/Australia/Dubai", multiple return visits; research-led.
- **Conversion trigger:** transparent explanations, cost logic (ranges not exact prices), destination logistics expertise.

### 3. Hybrid Assignee — Choice with Constraints
- **Funding:** company-funded within limits; cost-sensitive; approval-dependent.
- **Profile:** age 30–49; origins London + regional UK hubs; often relocating with family.
- **Motivation:** stay within budget, avoid employer escalations, protect family experience.
- **Pain points:** cost overruns, confusing scope boundaries, "what's included" anxiety.
- **Website use:** reads service pages carefully; wants clarity on inclusions/optional extras; process over lifestyle.
- **Conversion trigger:** clear scope definition, evidence of working within corporate policy, ability to explain trade-offs.
- **Messaging:** "Flexible relocation solutions that work for you — and your employer."

### 4. Private Individual / Lifestyle Relocator
- **Funding:** fully private; highly cost-conscious.
- **Profile:** age 30–55; origins London, Cardiff; often moving partial household.
- **Motivation:** quality of life, simpler living, long-term relocation.
- **Pain points:** overwhelm, fear of expensive mistakes, not knowing where to start.
- **Website use:** deep content engagement, destination comparison, cost-of-living content.
- **Conversion trigger:** calm educational tone, "we'll guide you" positioning, ability to speak to a real person.

### 5. Research-Only Influencer
- May never convert but strongly influences decisions (partners, spouses, employees researching before involving HR/procurement).
- **Behaviour (GA):** huge organic search volume, very high page views, low direct enquiry rate.
- **Why they matter:** shape expectations, influence satisfaction, drive internal buy-in.

## Cross-persona insight (very important)
From Gerson's GA data:
- Most-viewed pages are **lifestyle and comparison content**.
- Users arrive **long before** any formal relocation conversation.
- The website acts as a **decision-shaping platform, not a lead form**.
- **Implication:** B2C personas are **top-of-funnel influencers**, not just buyers. Content and paid media should educate and reassure early, capturing demand before the RFP/enquiry stage.
