## 1. Google Ads conversion goal groups — the definition change

**2025 (1 Jan – 31 Dec 2025):**
| Group | Result | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 (secondary/soft actions) | **2,530** | Page views 1,261 · Phone call leads 833 · Get directions 300 · Contacts 123 · Sign-ups 13 |
| Group 2 (primary lead action) | **586** | Submit lead forms 586 |
| Group 3 (qualified — offline import) | **88** (value £44k) | Qualified leads 88 |

**2026 (1 Jan – 31 May 2026):**
| Group | Result | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| Group 1 (secondary/soft actions) | **1** | Phone call leads 1 · everything else 0 |
| Group 2 (primary lead action) | **124** (value £50k) | Submit lead forms 124 |
| Group 3 (qualified — offline import) | **0** | Qualified leads 0 |

**What this proves:**
- The ~2,121 "conversions" in the 2025 campaign export were mostly **Group 1 soft actions** (page views, phone clicks, get-directions) counted as conversions. This is why the 2025 blended CPA looked like ~£21.
- In 2026 those soft actions are switched off; the counted conversion is **Submit lead forms** (a genuine enquiry). This is why the 2026 CPA looks like ~£170.
- **The genuine like-for-like lead metric is "Submit lead forms":** 586 (2025 full year, ≈48.8/mo) vs 124 (2026 Jan–May, ≈24.8/mo).
- **Group 3 (Qualified leads / offline import) was active in 2025 (88, £44k value) but is 0 in 2026** — the Moveware→Google Ads offline conversion import is not running in 2026. Re-enabling it would let Smart Bidding optimise toward booked revenue, not just form-fills. (Recommendation for the "How".)

## 2. Moveware funnel — Conversion Quality (KPI 3)

Source: AGM Group Consolidation Power BI, Brand = Abels Moving Services, by Entered Date. **All lead sources** (webforms, phone, etc.) — not paid-media only. Treat as whole-business quality context.

| Metric | 2025 (full year) | 2026 (Jan–May) |
|---|---|---|
| Total leads | 1,731 | 806 |
| Quoted leads (>0) | 575 | 216 |
| Lead → opportunity conv. | 33% | 27% |
| Total revenue opportunity | £3,236,515 | £1,232,588 |
| **Jobs won** | **174** | **53** |
| % of revenue won | 30% | 24% |
| **Won revenue** | **£963,992** | **£291,919** |
| Won est. margin | £301,972 | £112,314 |
| Won est. GM% | 31.3% | **38.5%** |
| Quoted conv% | 30.3% | 24.5% |
| **Won-job conv% (lead→booked job)** | **10.1%** | **6.6%** |
| Avg won job value | £5,540 | £5,508 |
| Avg days to book | 41 | **21** |
| Avg job vol (cu ft) | 375.31 | 277.69 |

**Reading it (mind the period mismatch — 2026 is 5 months, 2025 is 12):**
- Monthly run-rates: leads 2025 ≈144/mo vs 2026 ≈161/mo (2026 slightly higher). Jobs won 2025 ≈14.5/mo vs 2026 ≈10.6/mo.
- **Quality signals improving:** gross margin 31.3% → 38.5%; avg days to book 41 → 21 (faster). Avg job value steady (~£5,500).
- **Won-job conversion lower in 2026 (6.6% vs 10.1%)** — flag cautiously: partly genuine, partly pipeline lag (some recent leads not yet booked, though short days-to-book limits this).
- Avg job value ~£5,500 lets us contextualise paid CPL against revenue: a lead-form CPL of ~£130–170 against an ~£5,500 average won job at ~38% margin is commercially strong **if** close rates hold.

**Caveats:** Moveware leads are all-source (not attributable to paid media alone). 2026 is a partial year. Won-job figures may firm up as the pipeline matures.
