## ⚠️ Definitions & caveats (read first — these change how the numbers compare)

1. **2026 conversion-tracking change.** From Jan 2026, Google Ads stopped counting "soft" actions (page views, phone-click, get-directions) and counts only **genuine lead-form submissions**. So:
   - **2025 "conversions" are inflated by soft actions** (this is why 2025 CPA looks like ~£20).
   - **2026 "conversions" are real enquiries** (CPA ~£160).
   - **→ Conversion COUNT and CPA are NOT comparable year-on-year.** Spend, clicks and impressions *are* comparable.
2. **Performance Max** ran Jan–Feb 2026 only (£1,010 spend, **0 lead-form conversions**), then was paused. It also ran throughout 2025. PMAX inflates impression counts massively (display/discovery).
3. **Named search campaigns** (Europe, London, Domestic, International, Brand) only carry data from **March 2026** onward — the account was rebuilt then (JDR → Woody transition). Jan–Feb 2026 paid spend sits under the older structure.
4. **SEO = organic search only** (Google Search Console). Separate from paid.
5. **Attribution gap:** paid enquiries are tracked; **organic/direct enquiries are NOT yet attributed** (needs a GA4 channel-split export). SEO figures below are clicks/impressions/rankings, not leads.
