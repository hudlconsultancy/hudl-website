**Date:** 4 June 2026
**Source:** Google Ads Campaign report — `abels-google-ads-campaign-report-2026-01-01_2026-05-31.csv`
**Period:** 1 January – 31 May 2026 · GBP · Search campaigns (JDR Group)

## Headline
Search is **working at a blended £170 CPA and 9.6% conversion rate** — strong for high-value removals. But the spend is **mis-weighted**: the worst-performing campaign (Domestic) is the biggest line item, while the two best (Europe, London) are throttled by budget. Rebalancing is the single biggest opportunity.

## 🔴 Fix — Domestic (Removals & Packing)
- **36% of total spend (£5,979) for only 21% of conversions.** Worst CPA (£299, ~2.3× the account average) and worst conv. rate (5.09%).
- Only campaign returning **less in Google-attributed value than it costs** (0.74×).
- **Actions:** mine the search-terms report and add negatives (likely broad/irrelevant domestic queries), tighten match types, and review the domestic landing page / quote form. Lower its Target CPA and **reallocate freed budget to Europe & London** rather than cutting domestic out (it's core, high-value business — the issue is query quality, not the segment).

## 🟢 Scale — Europe, London (and protect Brand)
- **Europe (Outbound):** best volume *and* efficiency (47 conv, £130 CPA, 2.57× value). Losing **44%** of impression share to budget — raise the daily budget to capture it.
- **London:** healthy £151 CPA but losing a huge **63%** of impression share to budget — the most constrained winner. Increase budget.
- **Brand:** tiny £72 spend, 5.63× value, near-zero lost IS — keep always-on; a small uplift is low-risk.
- **International:** efficient (2.03×) but small; test a modest budget increase.

## ⚠️ Note on the pipeline / ROAS figures
The dashboard's **Targets** tab shows a pipeline of **£555,848** and **33.8× ROAS**. That uses HUDL's accumulator method (96.89 conv × £5,737 avg. move value) and assumes conversions become booked jobs — it is **indicative pipeline, not confirmed revenue**. Google's own attributed conversion value for the period is **£28,642 (1.74×)**. Booked-job/revenue truth still needs the **Moveware** export to confirm close rates.

## Priority actions (by £ impact)
1. **Reallocate budget** from Domestic → Europe & London (both budget-limited winners).
2. **Clean up Domestic** — negatives, match types, landing page — to pull its £299 CPA toward the £170 average.
3. **Raise Europe & London daily budgets** to recover lost impression share (44% / 63%).
4. Get **Moveware** booked-job data to validate pipeline vs. real revenue.
