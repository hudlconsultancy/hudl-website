# Abels Moving Services — Marketing KPI Summary
### January–May 2026 · One-page board view

> **Internal — not for external distribution.** Sources: Google Ads, GA4, Google Search Console, Moveware/AGM Power BI. No figures invented; all from data on file.

---

**1 · Website Visitors — top-of-funnel traffic**
**15,341 website sessions** (≈12,600 unique visitors) Jan–May, of which **~7,510 are intent-driven search visits** — 3,142 from paid search + 4,368 from organic search. Monthly sessions eased from a January seasonal peak (3,943) to a steady ~2,600–2,850, and **engagement quality rose to its best level in May**. Volume is below January and below last year by design — we switched off Performance Max and stopped counting low-intent "soft" traffic — so the funnel is **smaller but more relevant**. *Growth lever: organic holds 1.24m search impressions but ranks page 2–3 — lifting the commercial pages is the clearest route to more relevant traffic.*

**2 · Conversion Volume — enquiries generated**
**124 genuine lead-form enquiries from paid search** (136 attributed conversions in Google Ads), worth ~£50k of tracked enquiry value. Across the **whole business and all sources**, Moveware recorded **806 leads** in the period (≈161/month, slightly ahead of 2025's run-rate). Paid volume stepped up after the March account rebuild — April and May each delivered ~37 enquiries.

**3 · Conversion Quality — leads meeting qualification**
Of the 806 all-source leads, **216 (27%) progressed to a quote/opportunity** and **53 became booked jobs (6.6% lead-to-job)** at an average value of **£5,508** and a **38.5% gross margin** (up from 31.3% in 2025). Quality is therefore **strong and improving on value**, with average time-to-book down from 41 to 21 days. ⚠️ One gap: the **Moveware → Google Ads qualified-lead import is switched off in 2026** (88 qualified leads fed back in 2025, 0 now) — re-enabling it lets Google optimise toward booked revenue, not just form-fills.

**4 · Cost per Lead (CPL)**
**£172 blended cost per enquiry** across the period, settling to **~£160 in April–May** — the true post-rebuild run-rate. By campaign: **Europe £111–177 and London £125–208 are the most efficient** (and still budget-limited), International £120–182, while **Domestic is the outlier (£199–436) and is being addressed** — its May CPL of £199 shows the clean-up working. Against a £5,508 average won-job value at 38.5% margin, a £130–170 CPL is commercially sound where close rates hold.

**5 · Marketing Spend vs Budget — control & efficiency**
**£23,375 spent against a £200/day ceiling** (≈£30,200 available over 151 days) — i.e. **77% of budget used, comfortably within control.** Spend was deliberately low in Jan–Feb during the transition, then ramped: **May ran at ~£191/day, just under the cap.** Efficiency improved as spend rose — CPL fell from £228 (March) to ~£160 (April–May) — so we are **spending more while paying less per lead.** Headroom remains to scale the efficient Europe/London campaigns within the existing ceiling.

---

**Bottom line for the board:** paid search is now efficient and honestly measured (~£160 per genuine enquiry), lead quality and margin are improving, and spend is well within budget with room to scale the best campaigns. Two priorities: **re-enable the Moveware offline-conversion import**, and **lift the organic commercial pages** to grow relevant top-of-funnel traffic.
