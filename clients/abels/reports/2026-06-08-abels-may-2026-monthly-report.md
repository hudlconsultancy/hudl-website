# Abels Moving Services — Monthly Marketing Report
### May 2026 · with Jan–Apr 2026 trend and May 2025 (YoY) comparison

> **Internal — not for external distribution.** Reviews May 2026, trended against the previous four months (Jan–Apr 2026) and compared year-on-year against May 2025. Sources: Google Ads, GA4, Google Search Console, Moveware/AGM Power BI. No figures invented.
>
> ⚠️ **Read first — the YoY conversion caveat.** In 2026 the Google Ads conversion definition was changed: "soft" actions (page views, phone clicks, get-directions) are no longer counted, leaving only **genuine lead-form enquiries**. May 2025's "123 conversions at £24.61" were *mostly soft actions*; May 2026's "37 at £160" are *real enquiries*. **Conversion count and CPL are therefore NOT like-for-like year-on-year** — spend and traffic are comparable, conversions are not. Flagged throughout.

## 1 · Website Visitors — top-of-funnel traffic

**May 2026: 2,847 sessions · 2,478 active users · 2,238 new users · 43.2s avg engagement.**

| | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | **May** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Sessions | 3,943 | 3,161 | 2,814 | 2,576 | **2,847** |
| New users | 3,187 | 2,433 | 2,156 | 2,047 | **2,238** |
| Avg engagement (s) | 42.6 | 46.0 | 48.2 | 43.1 | **43.2** |

**Trend (Jan–May):** traffic eased from the January seasonal peak to a March–April low, then **ticked back up in May (+10.5% on April)** — the first monthly rise of the year. Engagement time has held in the 43–48s band throughout.

**YoY (May 2025 → May 2026):** sessions **4,517 → 2,847 (−37%)**; active users 3,918 → 2,478 (−37%). **This is deliberate, not underperformance** — last May the account was running Performance Max (heavy low-intent display traffic) and counting soft actions. With those removed, the funnel is smaller but **more relevant**: average engagement actually *rose* YoY (40.4s → 43.2s).

## 2 · Conversion Volume — enquiries generated

**May 2026: 37 genuine lead-form enquiries from paid search.**

| | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | **May** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Paid enquiries | 28.9 | 9.0 | 23.0 | 37.9 | **37.0** |

**Trend:** enquiry volume collapsed in February (transition/PMAX wind-down), recovered through the March rebuild, and has **settled at ~37/month in April–May** — the stable post-rebuild run-rate.

**YoY:** May 2025 recorded 123 "conversions", but those were overwhelmingly soft actions, not enquiries — **not comparable**. The genuine like-for-like metric (Submit lead forms) ran at **~49/month across 2025** vs ~37 in May 2026. Lower in count, but each is now a verified enquiry rather than a page-view, and Moveware shows the all-source lead run-rate is actually slightly *ahead* of 2025 (≈161/mo vs ≈144/mo).

## 3 · Conversion Quality — leads meeting qualification

**Latest position: 27% of leads progress to a quote, 6.6% become booked jobs, at 38.5% gross margin.**

| Quality metric | 2025 (full year) | 2026 (Jan–May) | Direction |
|---|--:|--:|:--:|
| Lead → quote/opportunity | 33% | 27% | ↓ |
| Lead → booked job | 10.1% | 6.6% | ↓ (partly pipeline lag) |
| Avg won-job value | £5,540 | £5,508 | → steady |
| **Gross margin %** | 31.3% | **38.5%** | **↑** |
| Avg days to book | 41 | **21** | **↑ faster** |

**Read:** quality is **improving on the measures that matter commercially** — margin up 7 points and time-to-book halved. The lower lead→job rate is partly genuine and partly pipeline lag (recent leads not yet booked). *Note: Moveware figures are all-source and can't be isolated to May or to paid media; 2026 is a partial year.* ⚠️ The **Moveware → Google Ads qualified-lead import remains off** (88 qualified leads fed back in 2025, 0 in 2026) — re-enabling it would let Google optimise toward booked revenue.

## 4 · Cost per Lead (CPL)

**May 2026: £160 per enquiry (blended).**

| | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | **May** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Blended CPL | £154 | £181 | £228 | £161 | **£160** |

**Trend:** CPL peaked in March during the rebuild (£228) and has held at **~£160 for two straight months** — the account's true efficient run-rate. Best campaigns in May: **Europe £128, London £138, International £182**; Domestic remains dearest but **improved sharply to £199** (from £436 in April) as the clean-up took effect.

**YoY:** May 2025's £24.61 included soft actions and is **not comparable**. Measured properly on genuine enquiries, £160 against a £5,508 average won-job value at 38.5% margin is a commercially sound cost of acquisition.

## 5 · Marketing Spend vs Budget — control & efficiency

**May 2026: £5,929 spent = ~£191/day, just inside the £200/day ceiling.**

| | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | **May** |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Spend | £4,470 | £1,631 | £5,242 | £6,103 | **£5,929** |
| ≈ £/day | £144 | £58 | £169 | £203 | **£191** |

**Trend:** spend was deliberately throttled during the Jan–Feb transition, then scaled to the ceiling from March. **Crucially, efficiency improved as spend rose** — CPL fell from £228 to ~£160 while monthly spend roughly tripled off the February low. May sat at 96% of the daily cap with full control. (April nudged the cap at £203/day — worth a light watch.)

**YoY:** spend **£3,027 → £5,929 (+96%)** — we are investing roughly twice as much as last May, but into a controlled, honestly-measured account producing verified enquiries rather than soft-action volume.
