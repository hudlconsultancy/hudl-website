## 1. Paid Search (PPC)

**The numbers:** £23,375 spent · 136 enquiries · £172 average cost per enquiry · £43,967 in tracked enquiry value (1.9× return on spend in Apr–May).

> **WHAT we did** — Rebuilt the Google Ads account in March around four clear campaigns (Europe, London, Domestic, International), switched off Performance Max, and brought management in-house during the JDR review.
>
> **WHY we did it** — Performance Max had spent £1,010 over January–February and produced **zero enquiries**. The old setup also counted soft actions (page views, phone clicks) as "conversions", which flattered the numbers. We wanted real enquiries, properly measured, in campaigns we could control.
>
> **HOW it's going** — April and May are the true picture: ~£6k a month, ~37 enquiries, **~£160 each**. Europe and London are the strongest (£110–138 per enquiry) and still have room to grow; Domestic is the most expensive but improving (£436 → £199 in May). **Next:** move more budget into Europe & London, keep tightening Domestic.

## 2. Website

**The numbers:** 15,341 visits · 12,061 new visitors · engagement time steady at ~44 seconds · best engagement of the period in May.

> **WHAT we did** — Tracked website traffic and engagement month by month alongside the paid and organic activity.
>
> **WHY it matters** — The website is where every enquiry is made, so its traffic and engagement are the bridge between marketing spend and leads.
>
> **HOW it's going** — Traffic eased from a January peak (~3,900 visits) to a steady ~2,600–2,850 a month. That's expected — we stopped buying low-intent Performance Max traffic and moved away from chasing soft clicks. Importantly, **engagement held up and hit its best level in May**, so the visitors we keep are more engaged. **Next:** confirm exactly which on-site actions we count as enquiries, and supply a channel-split export so we can see paid vs organic vs direct in one place.

---

## 3. SEO (Organic Search)

**The numbers:** 4,368 clicks from **1,242,569 appearances** in Google search · 0.35% click rate · average position ~25 (page 2–3).

> **WHAT we found** — Abels appears in Google an enormous amount (1.24 million times in five months) but earns relatively few clicks, because most pages rank on page 2–3 rather than page 1.
>
> **WHY it matters** — The demand is already there — people are searching for exactly what Abels does. The big commercial pages (international removals, London removals, London storage) hold **27% of all our search visibility** but rank on page 3–5, so they earn almost no clicks. Today, **42% of organic clicks are just the homepage** — i.e. people already searching for "Abels" by name.
>
> **HOW we improve it** — Focus SEO effort on lifting those high-visibility commercial pages from page 3–5 onto page 1–2. The demand is proven; the rankings are the gap. Our content (moving guides, country guides, the Royal Warrant page) already ranks well and supports credibility. **Next:** a focused SEO push on the London / international / storage pages.
