## 1. Objective & KPIs

**Primary objective:** Increase total qualified removals enquiries in Q3 by **reallocating budget from the loss-making Domestic campaign into the budget-throttled, profitable Europe and London campaigns**, while pulling Domestic's economics back toward the account average — all at or below the **~£170 blended CPA**.

One primary goal: **more qualified enquiries at or below £170 blended CPA.**

## 2. Audience

The website is overwhelmingly a **first-touch acquisition channel**: ~12,633 active users Jan–Jun, ~90% new visitors, short ~44s sessions, ~10% return rate (GA4). People move infrequently, so there is little repeat traffic to lean on — **acquisition does the heavy lifting** and the on-site journey must convert quickly.

Three priority segments, ranked by proven commercial value:

1. **Europe (Outbound) movers — PRIORITY 1.** UK households/individuals relocating to mainland Europe. Best volume *and* efficiency (47 conv, £130 CPA, 2.57× value) yet starved of budget (44% lost IS). High intent, high move value. **Insight: demand exists and converts — we are simply not showing up.**
2. **London movers — PRIORITY 2.** Moves within/into/out of Greater London. Efficient (£151 CPA, 1.83×) but the most throttled winner (63% lost IS). Dense, competitive, high-value market. **Insight: the biggest single pool of missed impressions in the account.**
3. **Domestic (Removals & Packing) movers — PRIORITY 3 (fix, don't abandon).** Core UK home-removals demand. Currently the worst performer (£299 CPA, 5.09% conv, 0.74×) — but the diagnosis is **query quality, not the segment**. **Insight: broad/irrelevant queries and a weak quote path are burning budget; tightening targeting + landing page should recover it.**

Protect always-on: **Brand** (£24 CPA, 5.63×) and **International** (2.03×, efficient but small — modest test).

## 3. Positioning & messaging

**Core promise:** *The art of moving* — Abels as the trusted, established mover that removes the stress from a complex move, with the care and experience to get it right.

**Proof points (use honestly; quoted figures need a real source):**
- Established, experienced removals & relocation specialist.
- Specialist European and international relocation capability (not just a man-and-van).
- Care, packing expertise and reliability — reassurance for high-value, emotionally-charged moves.

**Guardrails (from profile):** lead with reliability, care and experience. **Do not over-promise timescales or prices.** Use ranges, never guarantees. Any quoted price/saving needs a verifiable source.

### Per-segment angles

| Segment | Angle | Example message (illustrative) |

| Domestic | Reliability and care for your home move (tighter, intent-led) | "Your home move, handled with care by an established UK mover." |
| Brand | Reassurance / capture existing intent | "Abels — the art of moving." |

## 4. Channel plan

Q3 is **paid-search-led** (where the proven, profitable demand already is), with a focused **conversion-rate (CRO)** workstream and a **light content/SEO** layer to support quality scores and longer-term organic. We are **not** opening expensive new channels mid-quarter; we double down on what works.

| Channel | Role in campaign | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid Search (Google Ads)** — primary | Scale Europe & London; fix/contain Domestic; protect Brand; test International | This is where qualified, high-intent removals demand converts today and where budget is being mis-spent. Single biggest lever. |
| **Landing-page / quote-form CRO** — primary support | Improve quote-request completion, especially Domestic & Europe pages | GA4 shows ~44s sessions and a low key-event rate; even small conversion lifts compound the paid spend. |
| **Content / SEO** — supporting (light) | 2–3 intent-led guides (e.g. "Moving to Europe checklist", "London moving guide") to support ad quality + capture organic | Strengthens landing experience and Quality Score now; builds first-touch organic over time. Low cost, durable. |
| **Email / nurture** — minimal | Quote follow-up sequence for enquiries that don't convert immediately | ~90% new, ~10% return — there's little repeat traffic, so capturing and nurturing the enquiry is where retention value sits. |

Organic/paid social is **out of scope for Q3** (low-intent for infrequent, considered removals purchases) — revisit only if budget frees up and a brand-awareness objective is added.

**Net effect:** roughly **£900–1,200/month of Domestic spend reallocated to Europe + London.** Phase the shift (don't yank Domestic overnight) and let the Smart Bidding settle between moves. Decisions are data-led at each review, not fixed in stone.
