> British English. GBP throughout. No price claims, savings claims or guarantees (profile guardrail). All copy reflects "the art of moving" positioning: trusted, established, careful. Character counts shown in brackets after each asset — Google's limits are **≤30 chars per headline**, **≤90 chars per description**.

---

## How to use this document

- Each campaign has up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions.
- **Pinning recommendations** are noted per campaign — pin at minimum the intent-mirroring headline to Position 1.
- Add all three sets to the relevant Google Ads campaign as new RSAs; keep the existing ads running until the new ones accumulate statistically meaningful data (typically 2–3 weeks), then pause the weaker performer.
- Headlines marked **[PIN P1]** should be pinned to Position 1. Headlines marked **[PIN P2]** are recommended pins for Position 2.

## Flags for client/account manager sign-off before going live

1. **H10 "Trusted Since [Year]"** (Europe) — replace [Year] with Abels' verified founding year. If the year can't be confirmed from the client, remove this headline rather than estimate.
2. **H11 "Accredited Removal Company"** (all three campaigns) — only use if Abels holds a current, verifiable accreditation (e.g. BAR — British Association of Removers). Unverified accreditation claims in ad copy carry both a Google policy risk and a consumer-protection risk.
3. **D3 Europe — country list** ("France, Spain, Germany or beyond") — confirm these are destinations Abels actively serves before going live. If not, replace with "Moving to France, Spain or elsewhere in Europe?" or simply "Moving anywhere in Europe?".
4. **"Free survey"** in CTAs — the clean-up pack notes "free quote" should be confirmed as genuinely free. Confirm Abels offers a no-cost survey/quote before using this language in CTAs.
5. **All descriptions marked ⚠️** — use the revised (trimmed) versions. The originals are shown for reference only and must not be uploaded as-is.

## Implementation notes

- Upload as new RSAs alongside existing ads; do not immediately pause existing ads.
- Allow a minimum of two weeks for Google to serve impressions and gather headline combination data before judging performance.
- After the Domestic search-terms review (see clean-up pack §5), consider splitting top-converting exact-match queries into dedicated single-keyword ad groups with tightly matched H1s (e.g. "Removals and Packing" as both the keyword and H1).
- Review ad strength scores in Google Ads after upload; aim for "Good" or "Excellent" by ensuring diversity in headlines (avoid repeating the same words across multiple headlines in the same ad).
